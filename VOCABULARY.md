# ✦ SHINY EFFECT VOCABULARY

## Shine Carriers

Where brilliance lives on/in the object:

- **veins** — branching channels of optical activity
- **seams** — joins, stitches, sutures where materials meet
- **sutures** — surgical/repair stitches, ceremonial closing
- **channels** — conduits, pipes, routes for shine
- **filaments** — thin threads, fibers, hair-like light paths
- **capillaries** — tiny vessels, micro-routes
- **nerves** — signal-carrying shine, electric paths
- **roots** — underground/embedded branching systems
- **braids** — woven, interlaced, over/under structures
- **lace** — delicate openwork, ornamental filigree
- **welds** — joins, repairs, fusion points
- **cracks** — fractures, breaks, damage made bright
- **grout lines** — between tiles, tessellation seams
- **lattice edges** — grid boundaries, mesh perimeters
- **wireframes** — structural skeletons made of light
- **pressure ridges** — stress bands, compression glow
- **contour bands** — topographic, elevation-based shine
- **fracture halos** — glow around cracks, damage auras

## Shiny Materials

The optical substances themselves:

- **holographic foil** — diffraction, rainbow shift, thin-film
- **opal** — structural color, milky iridescence, internal fire
- **rhinestone** — faceted, tacky-glamorous, jewel flash
- **glitter** — particulate, scattered, microflake sparkle
- **mirror dust** — tiny reflective particles, mica-like
- **mica** — flaky, sheet-like, mineral shimmer
- **pearl powder** — soft, dusty, pearlescent haze
- **sequins** — disc-shaped, reflective, stitch-attached
- **metallic resin** — liquid metal, chrome lacquer, poured shine
- **chrome lacquer** — hard, reflective, mirror-like coating
- **oil slick** — thin-film interference, rainbow bands
- **diffraction film** — spectral split, prism-like color separation
- **candy shell** — hard glossy coating, lacquered surface
- **iridescent glaze** — ceramic, color-shifting surface film
- **fiber-optic thread** — light-carrying strands, glowing filaments
- **polarized acrylic** — stress-color, birefringent, rainbow pressure

## Matte / Dull Hosts

The quiet surfaces that make shine pop:

- **velvet** — absorbent, soft, deep, light-eating
- **clay** — earthy, granular, chalky, raw
- **chalk** — powdery, dry, porous, matte
- **plaster** — smooth, dead, architectural, pale
- **silicone** — skin-like, rubbery, translucent edge
- **ceramic** — hard, glazed or bisque, pottery
- **stone** — heavy, mineral, geological, ancient
- **ash** — gray, powdery, residual, burnt
- **felt** — fibrous, soft, compressed, textile
- **rubber** — elastic, matte, industrial, skin-like
- **latex** — shiny edge potential, skin-tight, membrane
- **paper pulp** — handmade, rough, organic, craft
- **suede** — napped, soft, directional texture
- **moss** — fuzzy, green, biological, damp
- **dry foam** — porous, light, crumbly, packaging
- **putty** — moldable, gray, temporary, filler
- **bone-like matte polymer** — medical, synthetic, pale

## Shine Behaviors

How the optical material acts over time:

- **pulsing** — rhythmic brightening, heartbeat, pump
- **branching** — splitting, growing, root-like spread
- **crawling** — slow movement, infection-like spread
- **pooling** — accumulation, wet gathering, lacquer
- **fraying** — degradation, worn edges, torn foil
- **woven** — over/under, textile rhythm, interlace
- **sutured** — stitched, repaired, tensioned
- **leaking** — escaping containment, buried breaking through
- **blooming** — growing outward, mold-like, reproductive
- **flaring** — sudden bright events, overload, spark
- **smearing** — wet drag, oil spread, glossy trail
- **flickering** — unstable, signal-like, nervous
- **rippling** — wave motion, caustic, pressure
- **banding** — stripe formation, stress, contour
- **clustering** — grouping, neighborhoods, density
- **crystallizing** — forming structure, frost, mineral
- **refracting** — bending light, prism, spectral
- **tearing** — ripping, foil, paper, edge events
- **sparkling** — glitter, sequin, microflash
- **ghosting** — faint, buried, subsurface, hidden
