## Specific Design Concepts

20 immediately buildable ideas:

1. **Velvet Tomb With Opal Root Veins** — Soft black velvet slab with root-like channels of buried opal light branching through it.
2. **Penrose Light Loom** — A Penrose tiling where every edge is a fiber-optic thread carrying different spectral color, woven into a dark felt ground.
3. **Glitter Capillary Porcelain** — Smooth bone-white ceramic with intricate branching capillary network of glitter resin under the glaze.
4. **Holographic Kintsugi Skin** — Matte skin-like latex membrane split by repaired cracks filled with diffraction foil.
5. **Caustic Quilt** — A stitched textile field where every seam behaves like a concentrated refractive caustic line.
6. **Mirror-Moss** — Matte mossy/fuzzy surface with tiny clusters of reflective foil scales blooming like spores.
7. **Rhinestone Nervous System** — A dark rubber or velvet ground with branching neural paths made from rhinestones, tiny and dense, with pulse-like glints.
8. **Candy Shell Fault Map** — A hard candy shell with tectonic crack systems glowing in color-shifting lacquer.
9. **Chromatic Suture Grid** — A surgical/embroidered-looking grid where crossings become jewel glints and the threads shift from teal to magenta to gold.
10. **Hologram Basket Weave** — A woven pattern that looks like strips of holographic foil threaded over/under each other, but with impossible shifting light.
11. **Opal Hex Pavers** — Hexagonal tessellations whose grout lines are dull, but whose interiors shimmer like crushed opal.
12. **Foil Bloodstream Marble** — Swirling marble-like matte substrate with thin electric channels of spectral foil threading through it.
13. **Prismatic Lace Armor** — Lace-like geometric filigree made from hard shiny light, floating over a soft muted underlayer.
14. **Glitter River Delta** — A matte desert-like surface cut by branching distributary rivers of dense glitter and reflective slime.
15. **Pearlescent Mold Filigree** — Pale mold/fungus shapes rendered with soft pearl instead of gross green fuzz, growing in decorative arabesques.
16. **Chrome Pollen** — A soft field dotted with pollen-like mirror particles that cluster into subtle generative formations.
17. **Spectral Vein Stone** — Rock or terrazzo surface with impossible interior veins of oil-slick spectral shine.
18. **Liquid Light Trellis** — A trellis or lattice structure that seems literally braided from wet colored light.
19. **Birefringent Tile Skin** — Clear-ish tile pattern where stress bands make each tile flash different rainbow pressure zones.
20. **Sequined Crackle Glaze** — Ceramic crackle-glaze logic, but every crack edge catches a tiny glitter spectrum.
