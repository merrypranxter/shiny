✦ SHINY

A generative art library for luminous, structured, optically alive creative code.
20 core systems · 45 showcase builds · shared utilities · doctrine

"Shiny design is not about covering a thing in gloss. It is about deciding where brilliance lives."
Shine is a structure — a seam, a vein, a crack, a woven path, a pressure ridge, a film on a dull host.
